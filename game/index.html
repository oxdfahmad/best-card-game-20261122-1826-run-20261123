A DUEL OF WIND-CALLERS

Call your wardens, ride the Surge, and take Mistral's life to zero.

🌀 Gale➜ 🍃 Bloom➜ 🌊 Tide➜ 🌀 Gale

Each turn you gain mana (1 more than last turn, up to 7) and draw a card. Play wardens into the three lanes, then end your turn: paired wardens trade blows, and a warden facing an empty lane strikes Mistral's life.

Wind advantage: a warden fighting the wind it beats strikes with +2 power. Surge: play two cards of one wind in a turn and all your wardens of that wind gain +1 power.

Spells: Gust wounds any warden, Rainfall mends yours (or heals you), Deep Draw draws two, Windfall grants mana, Cyclone storms every enemy warden.

Win by taking Mistral's life to zero. Drawing from an empty deck costs 2 life. The caller who moves second opens with a Tailwind — one free mana — to balance the first move.
